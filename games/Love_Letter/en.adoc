= Love Letter
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 16 character cards:
** 5 Guards (Wolf)
** 2 Priests (Monkey)
** 2 Barons (Snake)
** 2 Handmaids (Butterfly)
** 2 Princes (Dragon)
** 1 King (K ♠)
** 1 Countess (Q ♠)
** 1 Princess (Phoenix)
* Tokens to track round wins: numbered cards 1-7 in ★, or any other tokens.
****

In Love Letter, you are trying to deliver your love letter to the Princess.
To do so, you must get your letter into her hands while deflecting the letters from competing suitors.
The closer your letter is to the Princess, the better!


== Aim of the game

Be the last player remaining in a round, or hold the highest-ranked character when the deck runs out.
Win a number of rounds (tokens) to win the game:

[%autowidth,cols="^,^"]
|===
| Players | Tokens to win

| 2 | 7
| 3 | 5
| 4 | 4
|===


== Setup

1. Shuffle all 16 cards.
2. Remove the top card from the deck face-down without looking at it.
This card is out of the round.
3. *With 2 players only:* Remove 3 more cards face-up and set them aside.
These cards are visible to all players but out of the round.
4. Deal 1 card to each player.
This is your hand.
Keep it secret!
5. Place the remaining cards face-down as a draw deck.

| 8 | Princess | Phoenix
| 7 | Countess | Q ♠

== Gameplay

The player who most recently went on a date goes first (or choose randomly).
Play proceeds clockwise.

On your turn:

1. *Draw* the top card from the deck.
2. *Play* one of your two cards face-up in front of you.
3. *Apply* the played card's effect.

Your turn then ends, and play passes to the next player.


== Card effects

=== Guard (Wolf) - Rank 1
*Guess:* Name a non-Guard character and choose another player.
If that player has that character, they are *eliminated* from the round.

TIP: You cannot guess "Guard".

=== Priest (Monkey) - Rank 2
*Peek:* Choose another player and secretly look at their hand.
Do not reveal it to other players.

=== Baron (Snake) - Rank 3
*Compare:* Choose another player.
You and that player secretly compare hands.
The player with the *lower* rank is eliminated.
If tied, nothing happens.

=== Handmaid (Butterfly) - Rank 4
*Protection:* Until the start of your next turn, you cannot be targeted by any other player's card effect.

=== Prince (Dragon) - Rank 5
*Discard:* Choose any player (including yourself).
That player discards their hand and draws a new card.
If the deck is empty, they draw the face-down set-aside card.

IMPORTANT: If a player discards the Princess (Phoenix) this way, they are eliminated!

=== King (K ♠) - Rank 6
*Trade:* Choose another player and trade hands with them.

=== Countess (Q ♠) - Rank 7
*Forced play:* If you ever have the Countess *and* either the King or a Prince in your hand, you *must* play the Countess.

NOTE: The Countess has no effect when played. You may also choose to play her even if not forced.

=== Princess (Phoenix) - Rank 8
*Fragile:* If you play or discard the Princess for *any* reason, you are immediately *eliminated* from the round.

NOTE: The Princess is the highest-ranked card.
If you hold her at the end of the round, you win!


== Elimination

When you are eliminated:

* Discard all your cards face-up.
* You cannot be targeted by other players.
* You are out for the remainder of the round.


== End of round

The round ends when:

* Only *one player* remains (they win), or
* The *deck runs out* after a player's turn.

If the deck runs out, all remaining players reveal their hands.
The player with the *highest-ranked* card wins the round.
If tied, the player who discarded the highest total value during the round wins.

The winner takes a token of affection.


== End of game

The first player to collect the required number of tokens wins the game and the Princess's heart!
